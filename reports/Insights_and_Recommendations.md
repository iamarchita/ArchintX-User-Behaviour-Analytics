# Business Insights and Recommendations

## Project
ArchintX – AI-Powered User Behaviour Analytics

## Key Insights

1. **User Registration:** The dataset contains 120 registered users across different segments and acquisition sources.

2. **User Verification:** 102 out of 120 users were verified, representing 85% verification.

3. **Course Completion:** 69 out of 220 enrollments were completed, giving a completion rate of 31.36%.

4. **User Engagement:** October recorded 532 post-registration activity events from 114 active users, the highest among the observed months.

5. Retention: Week 1 retention was 76.67% across all registered users. Among fully observed cohorts,
Week 4 retention was 75.95% (60 out of 79 users).

6. **Segment Engagement:** Students recorded 335 post-registration activity events, the highest among the user segments.

7. **Course Performance:** Communication Skills had the highest enrollment count with 27 enrollments. Power BI had 8 completed enrollments.

8. **Acquisition:** College Community recorded a 90.48% user verification rate, the highest among the listed acquisition sources.

9. **User Inactivity:** As of November 8, 2026, 8 users had been inactive for at least 14 days, while 1 user had been inactive for at least 30 days.

10. **Platform Engagement:** iOS recorded 418 post-registration activity events, followed by Android with 391 and Web with 379.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Improve Course Completion:** Review course progress and learning engagement to identify where learners may need additional support.

2. **Strengthen User Retention:** Introduce onboarding guidance and timely engagement reminders to encourage users to return.

3. **Investigate Acquisition Quality:** Study differences in verification and enrollment rates across acquisition sources before allocating additional marketing effort.

4. **Target Inactive Users:** Consider re-engagement campaigns for users who have not performed qualifying activities for 14 or more days.

5. **Improve Course Engagement:** Review course-level enrollment, progress and completion patterns to identify opportunities for improving learning experiences.

## Conclusion

The analysis provides insights into user acquisition, engagement, retention and course performance. These findings can support data-driven decisions for improving platform engagement and learning outcomes.

*Note: These are observational findings and recommendations. They do not establish causal relationships.*
